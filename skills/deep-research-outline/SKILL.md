# deep-research-outline

Plan the research effort with structured collection strategies.

## When to invoke

- Workflow #4 enters PLAN phase
- Need to organize multi-source research
- Want to prioritize evidence collection
- Should allocate time/effort budgets per research question

## Phases

1. **Question Mapping**: Group related research questions.
2. **Source Categories**: Identify primary (filings, interviews), secondary (reports), and tertiary (commentary) sources.
3. **Collection Strategy**: Design search queries, archives to check, APIs to call.
4. **Time Budget**: Allocate effort per question (conservative estimate).
5. **Outline Output**: Write to `.opencode/tmp/research-outline-<project>.md`.

## Structure

Outline includes:
- Top-level research goal
- Grouped questions with priority tier
- Source categories and access constraints
- Collection queries and commands
- Milestone checkpoints and effort estimates

## Rules

- Outlines are estimates; adapt as information emerges.
- Collection strategies should leverage multiple source types to triangulate facts.
- Time budgets should include buffer for rabbit holes and dead ends.
